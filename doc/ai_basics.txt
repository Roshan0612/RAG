Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of building systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence.

This includes reasoning, learning, perception, language understanding, and decision-making. Early AI systems were rule-based and symbolic, but modern AI relies heavily on machine learning and deep learning.

Machine learning models are trained on large datasets to identify patterns and make predictions. Neural networks are especially useful for computer vision, speech recognition, and natural language processing.

A key challenge in AI is balancing generalization and overfitting. Good models learn meaningful patterns while staying robust on unseen inputs.
